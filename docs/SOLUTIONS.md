# Puzzle and ending rules

Each room chooses a server-only seed. Xorshift32 generates deterministic solvable settings. Solutions are absent from public snapshots. Roles are interchangeable; the operator cannot finish while the reference player is disconnected or away.

1. Split decoding: reader receives three glyphs. Operator legend is MOON=1, EYE=2, WAVE=3, THORN=4. Enter their digits in order.
2. Power routing: reader sees three cardinal directions. Operator cycles A/B/C clockwise through NORTH/EAST/SOUTH/WEST until all three match. Rotating A also rotates B; rotating B also rotates C. Work in order, accounting for the coupled circuits. The generator additionally requires the shared fuse.
3. Frequency: reader receives a target between 100 and 193 MHz. The reference player sets a coarse band (100/125/150/175 MHz); the receiver player tunes a fine offset (0–24 MHz). Their sum must equal the target. A tone accompanies tuning; the essential cue is also written.
4. Archive chronology: reader receives unsorted narrative timestamps and must determine chronological order. Operator legend maps Admission=1, Sedation=2, Broadcast=3, Evacuation=4. Enter the chronology as four digits. The shared keycard is required.
5. Traversal: reference player remains at (-8,-50), while the partner crosses to (8,-61) and latches the bridge. The far passage rejects movement when unattended; successful latching opens the permanent return route. Disconnecting cannot falsely retain a control.

Both maps use these mechanics with different scenery/story. Stage completion opens the next route. Optional hints arrive after two minutes without a system completion. Two powered lifts return from the side areas after stages 2 and 4.

Final intervention is at (0,-65). Both connected players must make explicit choices:

- **Containment**: five systems complete, both confirm source stabilization, then both choose contain.
- **Exposure**: five systems complete, at least three evidence records, source active (not stabilized or released), both choose expose.
- **Sacrifice**: five systems complete; one player explicitly chooses sacrifice for themselves, the other chooses escape (or also sacrifice). Neither operator can designate their partner as the sacrifice.
- **Outbreak**: five systems complete, both confirm source release, then both choose escape.

Release takes precedence over containment/exposure. Sacrifice takes precedence when voluntarily confirmed. Server integration tests verify each ending on both maps from controlled persisted fixtures. They do not establish playthrough duration or full uninterrupted navigation coverage.
